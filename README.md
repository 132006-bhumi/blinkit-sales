# 📊 Blinkit Sales Dashboard – Power BI Project
![Blinkit Logo](https://github.com/132006-bhumi/blinkit-sales/blob/main/Blinkit%20logo.jpeg)
# 🔍 Overview
This Power BI project presents an interactive dashboard analyzing the sales data of Blinkit, India’s last-minute delivery app. The dashboard provides key insights into sales performance, item categories, outlet types, fat content segmentation, outlet establishment trends, and regional sales breakdown. The aim is to assist business stakeholders in understanding where sales are concentrated, how different outlet and item types perform, and what influences average sales and ratings.
# 🎯 Objectives
• To analyze total and average sales across various outlet types and item categories.

• To compare sales based on fat content and outlet tiers (Tier 1, 2, 3).

• To evaluate outlet establishment trends over the years.

• To assess item visibility, customer ratings, and the distribution of products by outlet type and size.

• To enable filtering by outlet location type, size, and item type for deeper insights.
# ❓ Potential Questions for Analysis
1. Which item categories contribute most to total sales?

2. What is the distribution of sales between low-fat and regular-fat items?

3. How do outlet tiers (Tier 1, 2, 3) compare in terms of revenue generation?

4. Which outlet size (Small, Medium, High) generates the most sales?

5. How do different outlet types perform in terms of average sales and ratings?

6. In which year did Blinkit witness the highest outlet establishment performance?

7. What is the correlation between item visibility and average sales?

8. Are customer ratings consistent across outlet types and sizes?
# 📌 Findings and Conclusion
• Total Sales amounted to $1.20M, with average sales of $141 per item and a total of 8523 items.

• Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods are the top-selling item categories (~$0.18M each).

• The majority of sales come from Regular fat content items ($776.32K), with Low Fat items contributing $425.36K.

• Tier 3 locations generated the highest revenue ($472.13K), followed by Tier 2 ($393.15K) and Tier 1 ($336.40K).

• Most sales came from Supermarket Type 1 outlets ($787.55K), suggesting these stores have high product availability and visibility.

• Outlet establishment peaked in 2018 with $205K sales but declined afterward.

• High-sized outlets accounted for the highest revenue ($507.90K), indicating size positively influences performance.

• Ratings across outlet types are consistent (~4.0), indicating customer satisfaction is steady regardless of outlet type.

# Conclusion:
Sales performance is significantly influenced by outlet type, location tier, and item category. Supermarket Type 1 and high-sized outlets in Tier 3 cities present the most opportunity for revenue growth. A consistent rating across outlet types suggests stable customer satisfaction, while high sales from regular-fat products highlight consumer preference trends.
